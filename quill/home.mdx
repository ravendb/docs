## How it works

Quill reads only the tables you select and keeps a mirrored copy of them current as your data changes. Agents
answer from that copy, so your own database goes on serving your applications exactly as before.

## Three steps to a working chat

After you [sign up](getting-started/signing-up.mdx) and [start the container](getting-started/starting-quill.mdx),
three wizard-guided steps take you to a chat that answers your users. Quill drafts the table mapping, the agent,
and its queries; you approve or adjust each draft.
