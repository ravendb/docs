# Guide CTA footer

Every hosted guide ends with the same call to action block: the Discord community, a free developer
license, a free RavenDB Cloud database, and the rest of the guides.

## How it works

- `src/theme/DocItem/Footer` renders `GuideCtaFooter` under every page of the `guides` plugin.
- The guides home page and external guides (`external_url` in frontmatter) don't get it.
- It sits right after the content, before the `see_also` links.
- The copy and links live in `src/components/Guides/GuideCtaFooter.tsx`. Change them there and every
  guide picks up the change.

<Admonition type="warning" title="Don't write your own CTA paragraph">
    End the guide on its own takeaways: a summary, or links to related guides and docs. Don't add
    "Join our Discord", "Grab a developer license" or "Get a free cloud database" paragraphs; the footer
    already covers them, right below your last paragraph.
</Admonition>
