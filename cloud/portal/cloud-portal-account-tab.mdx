### Billing information management

In this section you can edit the data that will be used for the invoice.

![Account Tab - Edit invoice information](./assets/portal-account-invoice-information-edit.png)

### User management

Managing account users — inviting or removing members, assigning their [account roles](../../cloud/cloud-account.mdx#account-roles), and managing their [per-product permissions](../../cloud/cloud-account.mdx#product-permissions) — is now done from the dedicated [Users & Access](../../cloud/portal/cloud-portal-users-and-access-tab.mdx) page.

### Account features

Features that apply to the whole account are set up from the separate **Account features** view in the sidebar.
Each feature has its own card:

* **External SSO & User Management**: single sign-on through your organization's identity provider.
  See [External SSO & User Management](../../cloud/cloud-sso.mdx).
* **Sign-in methods**: the sign-in methods the members of this account can use.
  See [Sign-in methods](#sign-in-methods) below.

Each card is shown only to users with a matching [account role](../../cloud/cloud-account.mdx#account-roles): the
*Sign-in methods* card requires the **Sign-in methods** role. A card reads *Configured* once its feature is set up.

![Account features](./assets/portal-account-features.png)

#### Sign-in methods {#sign-in-methods}

By default, members of an account can sign in with any method the
[login page](../../cloud/cloud-overview.mdx#login-to-your-account) offers. The **Sign-in methods** page lets you block
any of these methods for your account:

* **Email link** (the magic link)
* **Google**
* **GitHub**
* **Microsoft**

[External SSO](../../cloud/cloud-sso.mdx) is not on the list and cannot be blocked.

![Sign-in methods](./assets/portal-account-features-sign-in-methods.png)

To block or allow a method:

1. Open **Account features** and click **Configure** (or **Manage**) on the **Sign-in methods** card.
2. Click **Edit** and turn off the switch of each method you want to block, or turn it on to allow it again.
3. Click **Save**. If you are blocking a method, confirm with **Block**: members signed in with that method lose
   access to the account immediately.

![Sign-in methods in edit mode](./assets/portal-account-features-sign-in-methods-edit.png)

![Block sign-in methods confirmation](./assets/portal-account-features-sign-in-methods-block-confirmation.png)

<Admonition type="note" title="The Account Owner is never blocked">
The **Account Owner** and RavenDB support can always sign in, whatever the list says. The owner's own login stays
the way back in if the account's sign-in setup ever locks the members out.
</Admonition>

Some switches cannot be turned off. Hover a disabled switch to see why:

* **Blocking all four methods** requires an active [External SSO](../../cloud/cloud-sso.mdx) connection, so that
  members still have a way in. With all four blocked, members can sign in only through SSO.
* **The method you are signed in with** cannot be blocked by you, unless you are the Account Owner.
  Sign in another way first.
* If the page asks you to **sign in again** before changing sign-in methods, your current session does not record how
  you signed in. Sign out and sign back in.

**What a member with a blocked method sees**

* If none of the member's accounts allows the method, signing in with it fails, and a member who is already signed in
  with it is signed out. The login page then says *Your account doesn't allow sign-in with GitHub. Sign in another
  way.*
* If another of the member's accounts still allows the method, the Portal switches to that account. In the account
  switcher, the account that blocks the method is greyed out with
  *Your sign-in method isn't allowed for this account.*
* A user who accepts an [invitation](../../cloud/portal/cloud-portal-users-and-access-tab.mdx#accepting-an-invitation) to an account can sign
  in with any method that account allows.
